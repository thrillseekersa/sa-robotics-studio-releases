Privacy Policy — SA Robotics Studio

Effective Date: November 2026  |  App Package: com.sarobotics.studio

Summary for Teachers, Parents & Students: SA Robotics Studio is a free, offline-first educational robotics simulator. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information, location data, analytics, advertising IDs, camera/microphone audio, or student accounts.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts, logins, names, emails, or student profiles are created or transmitted.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, crash telemetry SDKs, or analytics beacons are embedded in the application.
  • No camera or microphone access is requested or used.

2. Local Device Storage Only

Your robot builds, Word Block / Python programs (Slots 0–4), challenge high scores, tutorial progress, and simulator physics preferences are stored strictly inside local application storage on your tablet or computer so you can practice offline in classrooms without internet access. You can clear this data at any time inside Android App Settings or by resetting your tutorial/builder state in the app.

3. Optional Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Permission

If a student chooses to pair a physical 6-port classroom Smart Hub via Bluetooth Low Energy, Android asks permission for nearby Bluetooth devices (BLUETOOTH_SCAN with neverForLocation and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT; on older Android 11 or below devices, Android OS groups nearby BLE device scanning under Location permission). Location coordinates are never read, recorded, or transmitted off your device. The simulator works 100% offline without Bluetooth.

4. File Exports

When you tap Export Compatible .llsp3 Project or Download Python .py, the app creates a temporary project file and opens your device's system Save / Share sheet so you control where it is saved or shared.

5. Children's Privacy & Classroom Compliance

Because SA Robotics Studio collects zero personal data and includes no social feeds, in-app purchases, or external ad networks, it is designed to respect student privacy under POPIA, COPPA, and GDPR-K principles.

6. Educational Independence Notice

SA Robotics Studio is an independent educational tool designed to help schools train students in robotics mechanics, sensor engineering, and block/Python coding. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by third-party hardware manufacturers.
